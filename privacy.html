Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

CheatVault is an independent, unofficial reference app for cheat codes in supported Grand Theft Auto games. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app or contact us.

Privacy at a glance

CheatVault is designed to work offline, without an account, developer-operated analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking. Game preferences and favorites are stored locally on your device. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send.

1. Who We Are

CheatVault is an independently developed mobile application. For privacy inquiries, contact the application operator at teamcolicarthage@outlook.fr.

2. Information Collected Through the App

CheatVault does not require registration, a user account, or submission of a name, email address, or phone number to access its offline cheat-code library.

In the intended initial release, the app does not collect or transmit your game selections, favorites, or preferences to developer-operated servers.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

CheatVault may save information locally so that the app remembers your choices, including:

  • Selected game, platform, and edition.
  • Favorite or saved cheat codes.
  • App settings and preferences.
  • Onboarding completion status.

This information is used to provide app functionality and is not sent to our servers as part of the intended offline experience.

4. Offline Use and Internet Access

The bundled cheat-code library is designed to work without an internet connection. Browsing, searching, and accessing locally saved codes do not require online access. Opening external links or contacting support may require an internet connection.

5. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The intended initial release of CheatVault does not contain third-party advertising, developer-operated analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking technologies. We do not sell personal information or use locally stored game preferences for targeted advertising.

If these practices change in a future release, we will update this policy and the relevant App Store privacy disclosures as required.
